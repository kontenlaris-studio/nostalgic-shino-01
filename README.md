A childhood memory in one line: a VHS rental three days overdue, and a video store clerk still waiting for his rewind fee.

## Why this line

One concrete image — the overdue VHS, the rewind fee — carries the whole vanished world of video stores, and the joke lands on the clerk who never got paid. The memory is specific enough to be real and small enough to fit in one breath.
